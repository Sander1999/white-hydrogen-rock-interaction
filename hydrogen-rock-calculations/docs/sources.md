# Scientific basis

The inputs separate published physical relationships from illustrative parameters. The mineral recipes, reaction half-time, accessible fraction, activation energy/volume and mechanical constants are declared scenario choices, not fitted data.

- McCollom and Bach (2009), thermodynamic constraints on serpentinization and hydrogen: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gca.2008.10.032
- Klein, Bach and McCollom (2013), compositional controls and secondary mineral partitioning: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lithos.2013.03.008
- Kelemen and Hirth (2012), reaction-driven cracking: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.epsl.2012.06.018
- Evans, Spiegelman and Kelemen (2020), mechanically resolved phase-field treatment: https://doi.org/10.1029/2019JB018614. The simple screening index here is not that model.
- Geymond et al. (2023), magnetite alteration and metastable maghemite: https://doi.org/10.3389/feart.2023.1169356. The prescribed hematite endpoint is a declared alternative ideal balance, not a reproduction of those experiments.
- Ross et al. (2025; corrected 2026), olivine alteration under flow: https://doi.org/10.1029/2024GL114016. This supports discussing spatially variable reaction and permeability, not a universal permeability-increase rule.
- Parkhurst and Appelo (2013), PHREEQC version 3: https://doi.org/10.3133/tm6A43
- Zhang et al. (2020), SupPHREEQC database generation: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cageo.2020.104560. A consistent pressure-specific database is necessary before extending the equilibrium example to subsurface pressures.
- Handbook of Mineralogy, [forsterite](https://handbookofmineralogy.org/pdfs/forsterite.pdf) and [lizardite](https://handbookofmineralogy.org/pdfs/lizardite.pdf), density reference checks. Other model densities are explicitly nominal approximations.

Balanced endmember reactions and elemental accounting are derived in calculations.md. The low-LET radiolysis yield of 0.45 molecules per 100 eV is an illustrative parameter, not a universal yield for alpha radiation, brines or whole rocks. Deposited power must be supplied separately.
